# Automate Monad Blockchain Operations with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce that Velocity BPA has released a new community node for n8n that brings Monad blockchain automation to your fingertips!

## Why Monad Automation Matters

Monad is making waves in the blockchain space with its parallel execution capabilities, offering unprecedented performance for DeFi applications, staking operations, and governance activities. However, integrating Monad into your workflows typically requires custom development and deep blockchain expertise.

That's where our new n8n-nodes-monad community node comes in. Whether you're managing accounts, monitoring DeFi protocols, tracking staking rewards, or automating governance participation, this node eliminates the complexity of blockchain integration.

## Key Features

Our Monad node empowers you to:

- **Manage Accounts**: Create, monitor, and interact with Monad accounts directly from your n8n workflows
- **DeFi Operations**: Automate interactions with DeFi protocols on the Monad blockchain
- **Staking Management**: Monitor staking positions, track rewards, and automate staking operations
- **Governance Participation**: Stay informed about proposals and automate voting processes
- **Parallel Execution Support**: Leverage Monad's high-performance parallel execution architecture

All of this is available through n8n's intuitive visual workflow builder—no coding required!

## Getting Started

Installing the node is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-monad


After installation, restart n8n and you'll find the Monad node available in your node palette, ready to be dragged into your workflows.
